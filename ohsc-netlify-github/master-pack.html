OHSC Master Pack — What’s included

Governance

  • Practice Profile
  • Governance Policy
  • POPIA & Confidentiality Policy (paper/electronic/hybrid)
  • Patient Rights Charter
  • Complaints SOP + Register

IPC

  • IPC Policy
  • Hand Hygiene SOP
  • Cleaning SOP (standard/high-risk)
  • Waste Management SOP
  • Needlestick/Exposure SOP

Medicines

  • Medicines Management Policy
  • Dispensing SOP (variant)
  • Storage & Temperature SOP (if applicable)
  • Schedule 5/6 SOP + Register (if applicable)

HR & Safety

  • HR Policy + Induction + Training/CPD (if staff)
  • Fire & Evacuation Policy (single/multi-storey)
  • Disaster & Medical Emergency SOPs
  • Emergency equipment + fire/drill registers
